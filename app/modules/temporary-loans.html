Temporary Loans — Controlled Stores

ASO Pt 2 Ch 3 Temporary loans of controlled stores engraved with a ZA number (e.g. HP Intel Core Ultra 7 AIO desktop — ZA820) to an individual serving in a ZNA unit, formation, directorate, branch, or establishment. Return by the date on the loan voucher. Maximum authorised loan period: 2 weeks (14 days). Extensions: apply through Bde / Dist HQ two weeks before expiry. Cover internal issues / returns with ZNA Q 1033; keep a loan voucher receipt file.
