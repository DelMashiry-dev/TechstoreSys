ASO Ch 25 — Q records retention

Keep completed ledgers, vouchers, and backups for 3 years and/or only after Army Internal Audit before destruction. Export a Backup after major stock takes and month-end.

Tip: use START-SYSTEM.bat so data saves to techstores.db on disk.

Quick orientation

  • Dashboard top strip — IN-TRAY (Orderly Room, IT Dir, DP, POs).
  • Orderly Room (DF) — file letters & requisitions; alert TechStores so work is not piled up.
  • Unit Requisitions — action open requests; age shows in Alerts.
  • Universal Search — Ctrl+K to jump to modules, GLs, Q forms, dictionary terms, and to track issued controlled stores by ZA or Serial Number (shows current location / holder).
  • System Dictionary — definitions for every money, stock, form and role term (above).
