ZNA ICT Asset Register

Engraved equipment · Traceable expendables · Software renewals

ZNA ICT accounted by IT Dir. ZA numbers engraved at MLG (Masasa Logistics Garrison — Master Ledger). Printers / laptops / desktops are Serviceable (S) or Unserviceable (U/S). U/S items must be backloaded, struck off the unit or MLG ledger, then boarded. Also account for stolen or destroyed by natural causes. Forms: 1033, ZNA 982, ZNA/Q/1045, DP F1.
