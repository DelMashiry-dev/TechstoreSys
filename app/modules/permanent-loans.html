Permanent Loans — Laptops & iPads

Comd/34 · 06 Nov 15 Laptops and iPads are issued on a permanent loan (not the 14-day temporary loan). Eligible: Lieutenant Colonels and above holding command or staff appointments, and Grade Two Staff Officers at Formations and Army HQ. Training gadgets stay with the training institution / Training Branch. After 3 years from date of issue, IT Dir writes to QS Br for Masasa to scratch the ZA-NO and strike the item off the Master Ledger; then MID and IT Dir specialists erase military information before write-off as a personal item. Officers retiring within 3 years of issue must return the gadget. Those not issued have no claim on exit. Refs: Comd/34 (06 Nov 15) · AS(PLANS)/34 · IT/17 (15 Aug 18) · QM IT DIR 17 Mar 20.
