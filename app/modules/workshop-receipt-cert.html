Workshop Receipt Certification — ICT Deliveries

IT Dir Tech Stores workflow: Before ICT equipment is accepted into stock or sent to MLG (Masasa Logistics Garrison) for ZA engraving, the supplier delivery must pass through IT Dir Workshop for technical assessment against specification. When the delivery meets spec, complete the official Certification for Receipts of Goods and Services (Group 1 / 2 / 3 / VSD / RSD). The supplier then carries the signed certification together with the Purchase Order and Delivery Note to MLG to surrender the supply for engraving and/or storage.
