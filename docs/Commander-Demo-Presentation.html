Agenda

What we will cover

Briefing outcomes

What commanders should leave knowing

  • ICT equipment custody at the gate is recorded by RP without exposing ledgers.
  • TechStores staff alone alter stock quantities; Q / SVCS forms sit in one catalogue.
  • GL buying power is visible to command; oversight accounts are VIEW ONLY.
  • Data lives on disk in techstores.db — not only in the browser.
  • Orderly Room and Communications are scoped — non-stores staff do not browse GL stocks.
