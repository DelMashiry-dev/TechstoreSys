4. Operating rules (ASO-aligned)

  1. Every receipt/issue supportable by Q 982, Q 1033, or SVCS 890/1045.
  2. Stock takes → ZNA Q 987; surplus on charge; deficit under Ch 6.
  3. Loans returned by voucher date; extensions 2 weeks before expiry; Q 1033 for internal issues.
  4. Beyond local repair → 1045 → 1043 → 982 → strike off 1033.
  5. Local supplier: check DN → raise Q 1033 → attach to PO/invoice.
  6. Military consignment discrepancy → Q 985 (when available); do not casually amend vouchers.
  7. Retain completed Q records three years (Ch 25).
